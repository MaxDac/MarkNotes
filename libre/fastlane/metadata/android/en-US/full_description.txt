<p>MarkNotes is a simple Markdown notes app. Every note is a plain <code>.md</code> file in a folder tree on your device, so your notes work offline and stay readable by any other editor.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Folders: create, rename, move and delete notes and folders, with a drawer showing the whole tree.</li>
<li>Editor with a rendered preview (tables, task lists, syntax-highlighted code), a formatting toolbar and autosave.</li>
<li>Note links: type <code>@</code> to link to another note by title or ID. Links keep working when notes move.</li>
<li>Images from the gallery or the camera, stored once and shown in the preview.</li>
<li>Import from other apps: share text, web pages, Word documents, PDFs or images to MarkNotes to turn them into notes.</li>
<li>Chord sequences for guitar and bass with a fretboard chord builder.</li>
<li>Light, dark and Material You themes with eight accent colours.</li>
<li>Optional two-way sync with one or more Dropbox accounts: line-by-line merge of concurrent edits, conflicted copies when edits overlap and a screen to merge them back.</li>
</ul>

<p>The same notes folder works with the MarkNotes desktop and web apps.</p>

<p>Sync only runs when the app starts or when you tap <i>Sync now</i>; there is no background polling and no tracking. Sync uses Dropbox, a non-free network service, and is entirely optional.</p>
